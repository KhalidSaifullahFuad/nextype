AI autocomplete in any text field, running on your Mac. Needs Apple silicon and macOS 14+.

## Install

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag NexType to Applications.
2. Open NexType. When macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Allow Accessibility, relaunch, and let it download the model (2.8 GB).

**Tab** accepts a word, **`** accepts all, **Esc** hides.
